# Projects & Work

## Project 1: Agentic AI Voicebot — Trust & Reliability Lead
**Company:** American Express | **Timeline:** 2025–Present
**Scale:** ~1 million annual calls

### What I Built
I led the Trust & Reliability workstream for AmEx's first agentic AI voicebot — a conversational AI system handling real customer calls autonomously. This wasn't just product management; it was building the safety and reliability infrastructure that allows the company to trust an AI agent with actual customers in a regulated banking environment.

### The Four Workstreams I Owned
1. **Observability** — Defined the observability framework from scratch. Built 4+ dashboards tracking agent performance across IVR and voicebot pilots. If the agent misbehaves, we see it immediately.
2. **Data Ingestion** — Partnered with Tech to deliver both internal and external data ingestion pipelines ensuring the agent has clean, real-time context.
3. **Guardrails** — Partnered with enterprise Infosec and third-party vendors to define our guardrails strategy. Implemented prompt-based safety controls to prevent misuse and hallucinations.
4. **Red Teaming** — Coordinated multi-round adversarial testing on the voicebot before launch — including third-party red team assessment and experiential testing. Defined "safe to launch" criteria in partnership with enterprise Infosec.

### Team & Coordination
Led a cross-functional team of 4, coordinating across vendors, Tech, Analytics, and enterprise Infosec.

## Project 2: IVR LLM Pilot — Intent Classification
**Company:** American Express | **Timeline:** 2022–2025
**Scale:** ~80,000 annual calls across 4 TFNs

### What I Built
Supported end-to-end delivery of AmEx's IVR LLM pilot — replacing traditional ML-based intent mapping with LLM-based classification across 230 intents. Achieved 3–5% improvement in containment rate; call deflection patterns matched or exceeded the existing journey benchmarks.

### My Contributions
- Drove discovery, cost-benefit analysis (CBA), and the PRD for the Disambiguation Menu feature
- Managed rollout across 4 Toll-Free Numbers
- Synthesized pilot learnings that directly shaped Voicebot architecture and product decisions
