# Prerna's Philosophy & Goals

## The Thread That Connects Everything
I believe everything I've ever learned makes me better at what I do next. Nothing is wasted. I'm not a software engineer, but learning about APIs makes me a better PM than someone who doesn't. I'm not a data scientist, but building NLP models makes me think about AI products differently than someone who's never touched a dataset.

That compounding is deliberate. I chase adjacent skills because I know they'll connect eventually.

## Why Data Background Matters in AI Product
Most PMs rely on engineers to tell them when an AI system is broken. I don't have to wait for that. I can look at the signals myself — containment rates, escalation patterns, intent drift, pipeline failures — and make a plan before things fall apart.

In AI products, especially agentic systems, decisions happen autonomously at scale. A lot can go wrong quietly. Someone with data instincts can see the early signs. That's the edge I bring.

## What I Want to Build
I want to work on AI systems that are genuinely reliable — not just accurate in demos, but trustworthy in production. Systems where the safety and reliability work is treated as a first-class product problem, not an afterthought. I'm drawn to companies and teams that are wrestling seriously with the hard questions: How do you make AI systems behave as intended? How do you test for failure modes you haven't imagined yet? How do you build trust with users when the system is making autonomous decisions? These are product problems — and they're the ones I want to spend my career on.
