# My Product Philosophy

## Analytics-first, always
I came from analytics before product. That means I design metrics before I design features. I don't consider a launch complete until I know what "working" looks like in data.

## AI is a product, not a feature
Most teams bolt AI onto existing products. I've spent my career building AI as the core product — and that requires thinking differently about reliability, safety, and trust. An AI that's 95% accurate isn't good enough if the 5% failure mode is a customer being misled or harmed.

## Fast prototypes, real feedback
In the AI Labs context, I believe in building quick, scrappy prototypes that get real reactions from real stakeholders — not polishing ideas in slide decks. Show, don't tell.

## Safety is a product problem, not just an engineering problem
Red teaming and guardrails are often treated as engineering checkboxes. I believe they require product thinking — defining the threat model, understanding user intent, and deciding what tradeoffs are acceptable. This is where I want to grow as a PM.

## Build in public
This portfolio is the start of my public builder journey. I'm going to ship real projects — a fitness tracker, a transaction parser for Indian UPI data, a budgeting app — and document what I learn. Not because they'll all succeed, but because building is the best way to stay sharp.
