# About Prerna Gupta

## Who I Am
I'm Prerna Gupta — an AI Product Manager at American Express, currently part of the Global Servicing Innovation Lab. I'm an app geek who grew up obsessing over technology and turned that curiosity into a career. I've spent the last 6+ years building things at the intersection of data, product, and now AI. I didn't start in product — I started in analytics, which means I think differently about products: I ask "how will we know this worked?" before I ask "what should we build?"

My journey has taken me from building NLP models and BI dashboards for ecommerce clients, to owning full product delivery for AI systems that handle millions of customer interactions. Along the way I got curious about AI safety — and then got hands-on with it.

## My Arc
- **2019-2021:** Business Analyst at 1DigitalStack.ai — built dashboards and analytics for ecommerce clients (Philips, Saffola, Lotus). Learned to translate messy data into decisions.
- **2021-2022:** Integrated Systems Representative at Lutron Electronics — collaborated with PMs across the US/Canada on post-sales implementation. First exposure to PM thinking.
- **2022-2025:** Associate → Sr. Associate, Digital Product Analytics at American Express — deep product analytics on @Work, a corporate card platform with 1.5M clients and 3.5M users across 120+ countries. Built Inferno (NLP model), owned CSAT/revenue metrics frameworks, acted as PM for SpendSmart from discovery through launch (Feb 2024).
- **2025-Present:** Gen AI Product Manager, Innovation Lab at American Express — embedded in the AI Lab doing rapid concept testing and validation; leading Trust & Reliability for the company's first agentic AI voicebot; contributing to early-stage AI personalization concepts.

## What I'm Building Now
I've transitioned into AmEx's AI Labs — a sandbox environment where we test small-scale AI ideas, prototype fast, and decide whether to build or kill. Think: fast discovery, quick prototypes, real feedback loops. This portfolio is the first artifact of my public builder journey.
